You've Been Boo'd!

The air is cool, the leaves have dropped,
and on your porch a ghost has stopped!
Some sneaky friends came by tonight
to leave you treats and a little fright.

  1. Hang the ghost sign on your door so no one boos you twice.
  2. Gather two little bags of treats & make two copies of this card.
  3. Sneak them to two neighbors who haven't been boo'd.
  4. Knock or ring the bell… then run!

Shhh! Don't get caught & keep it a secret! 👻

To:
From: a friendly ghost
Happy Halloween!
Boo-ing ends Halloween night.
